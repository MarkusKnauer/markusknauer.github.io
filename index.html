Research Focus

I develop methods that enable robots to learn new skills interactively and adapt their behavior through natural language. A person shows the robot a movement a few times, and the robot turns those demonstrations into a skill it can adjust and reuse. My work combines probabilistic machine learning with foundation models — progressing from learning by physical demonstration, to language-guided motion adaptation, toward fully autonomous robot agents.

Key Results

2–5 demonstrations are enough to teach a new skill (LOCI, IROSA)
90–100% success rates after refining a skill interactively (LOCI, IROSA)
Adaptable skills transfer to new object positions and task configurations
Safe language models modulate the motion — they never control the robot directly
